What would carry into the game

Keep the shared construction: large body planes, cool glass, visible tires, thin trim, and warm headlights. Use fixed cracks and dents as damage increases, then add smoke near destruction. Patrol lamps stay steady.

The existing game has four car models and a separate Cybertruck. This exploration proposes five standard-car identities, an optional armored pickup, and a refined Tesla Cybertruck. Selection would require a deliberate model mapping; the armored pickup is not a silent replacement of the Cybertruck. Health, hit thresholds, scores, spawn rates, explosions, and special triggers are unchanged here.

Damage graphics, roof signs, and delivery lettering are proposed artwork. The condition selector previews visual stages, not exact hit counts. No external records or image assets are required. The coupe’s low silhouette needs collision-envelope review before implementation.

The yellow outline is the current obstacle envelope. On a wide display, one game unit equals one CSS pixel.
